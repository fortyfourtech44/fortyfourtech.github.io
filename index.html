About

About Forty Four Technologies

A small software studio that believes good software should be simple to use and honest about what it does.

Forty Four Technologies is an independent software company based in the Lakshadweep Islands, India. We build mobile apps that solve everyday problems for small businesses, families and individuals.

Our work starts with real needs: a shop that needs faster billing, a teacher who needs a clean fee register, a household juggling several loans. We design each app around the job it must do, test it in daily use, and improve it over time.

We care about the details that make software dependable: clear screens, honest data practices and updates that keep apps working as phones change. Our name comes from a number that guides us.

  • SimpleApps that make sense the first time you open them.
  • HonestClear about the data we handle and why.
  • DependableBuilt to work every day, not just in a demo.
  • LocalMade in Lakshadweep, for how people here and across India work.
