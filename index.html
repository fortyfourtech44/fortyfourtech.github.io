About us

A small studio with a simple belief: good software should be easy to use and honest about what it does.

We are based in the Lakshadweep Islands, India. We design, build and maintain our apps ourselves, from the first idea to updates on your phone.

Our work grows out of real needs we see around us, in shops, homes and small businesses.

  • Simple. Apps that make sense the first time you open them.
  • Honest. Clear about the data we use and why.
  • Reliable. Built to work every day, not just in a demo.
