Features

What Bilo does

Billing & repair management for mobile shops.

Billing

Pick items, add a service charge and bill the customer in a few taps.

Stock

See price, cost and stock for every item, with low-stock highlighted.

Repair jobs

Move each job through Received, Waiting for part, Ready and Delivered. Print a job card or send a reminder.

Customer credit

See who owes money and how much, with a clear customer list.

Money & accounts

Daily book, expenses, shop bills such as rent and electricity, and chitty tracking.

Service tools

Unlock job records and a warranty check by IMEI or phone number.

Your data

Where your data lives

Bilo keeps shop data in the cloud so it stays in sync and is not tied to one phone. Data is used only to run the shop's account. See our privacy policy.
