Features

What Kist does

Loans, EMIs and bills in one place.

This month

One card shows how much is cleared and what is still due.

Loans

Each loan shows instalments paid, the next date and the amount left.

Bills & subscriptions

Monthly, yearly or every-N-days bills, sorted by next due date.

Calendar

Tap any day to see what is due.

Insights

Your debt-free month, total paid, interest still to pay and a month-by-month outgo chart.

Backup

Export a backup of your data so you can restore it on another phone.

Your data

Where your data lives

Kist stores your data on your own phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us. You take and keep your own backups, and a cloud option may be added in the future. See our privacy policy.
