Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Forty Four Technologies ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile apps and on this website, fortyfourtech.in. It applies to all apps we publish, including Kist, Paadam and Kiswa.

Information we handle

The information an app handles depends on what the app does. For example, an app may let you enter names, phone numbers, amounts or dates so that it can work for you. Unless an app says otherwise, this information is stored on your own device and is not sent to us.

If an app uses an online service, such as sign-in, cloud backup or notifications, it will say so inside the app and in its Google Play listing. The Data safety section of each app's Google Play listing describes what that app collects and shares.

How we use information

We use information only to provide the features of the app you are using, to keep it working properly and to respond to your support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except where an app relies on a service provider to deliver a feature (for example, Google services used for sign-in or backup), or where the law requires it.

Permissions

Apps ask for device permissions (such as contacts, storage, camera or notifications) only when a feature needs them. You can change permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. If an app stores data online, you can ask us to delete it by writing to the email address below.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@fortyfourtech.in
