Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Forty Four Technologies ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile apps (Bilo, Kist and Paadam) and on this website, fortyfourtech.in. The Data safety section of each app's Google Play listing also describes what that app collects and shares.

Where each app keeps its data

Bilo

Bilo uses a cloud service to keep shop data in sync. The data you enter (such as bills, items, customers, repair jobs and expenses) is stored in that cloud account to run your shop's account and is used only for that purpose.

Kist

Kist stores your data (loans, bills, due dates) on your own phone. It is not uploaded to us. You are responsible for taking and keeping your own backups.

Paadam

Paadam stores student, parent, attendance and fee details on the teacher's own phone. It is not uploaded to us. The teacher is responsible for taking and keeping their own backups.

If we add cloud features to Kist or Paadam in future, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play listing before they take effect.

How we use information

We use information only to provide the features of the app you are using, to keep it working and to answer support requests. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties, except for service providers that deliver a feature (for example the cloud service used by Bilo), or where the law requires it.

Permissions

Apps ask for device permissions (such as contacts, storage or notifications) only when a feature needs them. You can change permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. For Bilo cloud data, write to the email below to ask for deletion.

Personal data of others

If you enter information about other people (for example customers, students or parents), please do so lawfully and keep it secure.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Paadam holds details of students entered by their teacher on the teacher's own device.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
