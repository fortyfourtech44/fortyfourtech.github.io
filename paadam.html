Features

What Paadam does

Fees and attendance for home tuition.

Monthly summary

See what is pending and what is collected for the month.

Students

Search by name, class or school. Active and inactive lists.

Pending fees

Each student's due month and amount, with Mark paid, Bill and Remind.

Attendance

A simple day-wise register with present, absent and not-marked counts.

Settings

Set the monthly fee, due day and a UPI ID for receipts.

Backup

Export a backup of your data to keep it safe.

Your data

Where your data lives

Paadam stores student and parent details on the teacher's own phone. Nothing is uploaded to us. The teacher keeps their own backups, and a cloud option may be added in the future. See our privacy policy.
